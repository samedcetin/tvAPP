# device
## SUMMARY
The target is the Xiaomi TV Box S (2nd Gen): model MDZ-28-AA, EU SKU PFJ4151EU, codename "jaws", device ID MiTV-AFKR0. It was announced in April 2023 and went on sale in Turkey around December 2023. It is a different product from the 2018 Mi Box S (1st gen, Android TV 8/9) and from the 2025 TV Box S 3rd Gen (MDZ-32-AA, S905X5M, 32 GB, Android TV 14).

Hardware:
- SoC: Amlogic S905X4, 4x Cortex-A55 at 2.0 GHz, Mali-G31 MP2 GPU (a very weak GPU).
- Memory: 2 GB RAM, of which only about 1 GB is free after boot.
- Storage: 8 GB eMMC with about 3.3–3.5 GB free. The eMMC is slow (about 5 MB/s random read).
- Connectivity: dual-band Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.2, HDMI (marketed as 2.1 but HDMI 2.0-class, 4K60 max), one USB 2.0 port (accepts a USB-Ethernet adapter), 3.5 mm audio out.
- Video: H.264, HEVC, VP9 and AV1 decode up to 4K60; Dolby Vision, HDR10 and HDR10+; Widevine L1.
- Audio: Dolby Audio/Atmos and DTS-HD passthrough.

OS:
- Ships with Google TV on Android TV 11, a 32-bit userspace build (RTT0.211222.001.7xx). The April 2025 security patch arrived as build .774 in June 2025.
- Community firmware trackers show Android TV 14 OTAs in the second half of 2025 (V816.0.25.8.20.1 and V816.0.25.10.28.UZFAABX). A given Turkish unit could therefore be on either 11 or 14 and must be checked.

Remote: Xiaomi Bluetooth + IR voice remote. It has Power, Google Assistant, a D-pad ring with centre OK, Back, Home, and Xiaomi TV+, Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube and APPS shortcut keys. Volume is a +/- rocker.
- It has no number keys, no CH+/CH-, no Mute, no Menu/Settings/Input/Guide/Info and no play/pause keys.
- The app can rely only on DPAD_UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT, DPAD_CENTER and BACK, including long-press.
- HOME, Assistant, Power and the branded shortcut keys are handled by the system.
- Volume keys are usually sent to the TV over IR/CEC, so the app should not repurpose them.

Sideloading:
- Enable Developer options at Settings > System > About > "Android TV OS build", pressing OK about 7 times.
- Then install through one of: Downloader by AFTVnews (URL or short code), Send Files to TV, a USB stick plus a file manager, or ADB.
- On first use Google TV asks you to allow each installer app under "Install unknown apps". Play Protect may show a "Blocked by Play Protect" prompt; the way past it is Details > Install anyway.
- ADB: on Android TV 11, USB debugging plus `adb connect IP:5555`. On Android TV 14 over Wi-Fi, Wireless debugging with a pairing code (supported on TV from Android 13).
- The APK must declare LEANBACK_LAUNCHER plus a 320x180 banner, or it won't appear on the Google TV UI. On Android 14 it must also target SDK 23 or higher.

Google's developer-verification enforcement (from 30 Sep 2026) covers only phones and tablets, only apps from "participating stores", and only in BR/ID/SG/TH. ADB installs are exempt. A free limited-distribution account (up to 20 devices) can future-proof the app for the 2027 global rollout.

## RECOMMENDATIONS
- Target minSdk 30 (the Android TV 11 launch build) and the current targetSdk (35/36), test on both the Android TV 11 (RTT0.211222.001.77x) and the Android TV 14 (V816.0.25.x) builds, and make no runtime use of APIs from API 31 and up (e.g. RenderEffect blur) without version guards.
- Ship a single universal release APK, not an AAB, signed with one permanent keystore (back it up), with versionCode increasing on every release. Enable R8 full mode plus resource shrinking, use vector icons, store channel logos as small WebP, and bundle no video or big fonts. Aim for a few MB to about 15 MB maximum, given 3.3 GB free on slow eMMC.
- Avoid native libraries (e.g. libVLC). Use Media3 ExoPlayer, which uses platform MediaCodec hardware decoders. If any native code is unavoidable, include armeabi-v7a because the launch firmware is a 32-bit userspace; verify with `adb shell getprop ro.product.cpu.abilist`.
- In the manifest: a LEANBACK_LAUNCHER activity, android:banner at 320x180 xhdpi with the Turkish app name in the image, uses-feature android.software.leanback, and touchscreen required=false. Also set configChanges keyboard|keyboardHidden|navigation so Bluetooth remote reconnects don't recreate the activity.
- Design input around only the D-pad, OK and Back:
- In the full-screen player, DPAD_UP/DOWN = previous/next channel (replacing the missing CH+/CH-), OK = open the channel list/info overlay, LEFT/RIGHT = category or quick list.
- Long-press OK = add/remove favourite.
- Back always closes the topmost overlay first and never toggles; repeated Back eventually exits, ideally with an 'exit?' confirmation for elderly users.
- Never rely on number, mute, menu, colour or media keys.
- Treat HOME, Assistant, Power, Volume and the Netflix/Prime Video/YouTube/Xiaomi TV+/APPS keys as uncontrollable escape hatches. On first install, put the app first in Google TV's 'Your apps'/favourites row (long-press OK > Move) so Home then one click brings parents back. Consider Google TV's apps-only/basic home mode to reduce clutter (unverified for this firmware).
- Optional, phase 2: an AccessibilityService with flagRequestFilterKeyEvents could remap one rarely-used shortcut key (e.g. Xiaomi TV+ or APPS) to launch the app. First verify the key reaches the accessibility layer, and logging what each key sends (`adb shell getevent -l` plus an in-app KeyEvent logger) must come first. It also requires the user to enable the service in Settings, so weigh that against the simplicity goal.
- Build a hidden diagnostics screen (e.g. reached by a long Back press on Settings) that logs every KeyEvent (keyCode, scanCode, repeatCount, source) and shows Build.VERSION, ABI list, ActivityManager.isLowRamDevice(), memoryClass, display mode/refresh rate and network type. This resolves the unverified keycode and OS questions on the real device.
- Respect the GPU and RAM budget:
- Solid dark surfaces, a focus state of scale about 1.05–1.1 plus a border or glow, no full-screen blur or translucency stacks.
- Standard Compose LazyRow/LazyColumn (not the deprecated TvLazy*) with stable keys; channel logos decoded at display size with hardware bitmaps.
- One ExoPlayer instance, no simultaneous live video previews, release the player in onStop.
- Baseline profiles; profile release builds only (debug Compose is much slower).
- Design canvas: 1920x1080 px UI, i.e. 960x540 dp at xhdpi, with about 5% overscan-safe margins (roughly 48 dp horizontal, 27 dp vertical). Video can be 4K, but the UI is rendered at 1080p.
- Default family install path: enable Developer options (Settings > System > About > 'Android TV OS build' x7), install 'Downloader by AFTVnews' from Play Store, host the signed APK at a stable HTTPS URL (e.g. GitHub Releases) with a short URL or AFTVnews short code, install, accept 'Install unknown apps' for Downloader, and choose 'Install anyway' on any Play Protect prompt. Afterwards delete the APK in Downloader to free storage. Document this in Turkish with screenshots.
- Developer install path: Android TV 11 = USB debugging plus `adb connect IP:5555`; Android TV 14 = Wireless debugging plus `adb pair`/`adb connect`. Use `adb install -r` for iteration and turn debugging off on the parents' box afterwards for security.
- Optional in-app updates: the app fetches a version JSON from your server, downloads the new APK and installs it via PackageInstaller. This needs the REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES permission and a one-time 'Install unknown apps' grant for the app itself. Signature and versionCode must match the installed app.
- Future-proofing: register the package name through a free Android Developer Console Limited Distribution account (up to 20 devices, no ID) before the 2027 global verification rollout, which may extend to TV. ADB installs remain exempt regardless.
- Ask the user to recommend a USB-Ethernet adapter, or at least 5 GHz Wi-Fi, for the box, since live HLS on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is the most likely source of buffering complaints.

## VERIFY MISSING
- Inattentive sleep. Android 11+ TV turns the screen off after a period with no user input, 'even if windows with FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON are visible' and even while screen wake locks are held (AOSP 'TV standby'). The timeout is set by Settings.Secure.ATTENTIVE_TIMEOUT. An elderly viewer watching live TV for hours without pressing a button will see the box warn and then sleep, and the app cannot stop it. The plan needs (a) a setup step to raise or disable the Energy saver / 'turn off display when inactive' timeout and (b) Turkish copy explaining the warning dialog. The exact menu path on this Xiaomi firmware is unverified; on Google TV it is typically Settings > System > (Power &) Energy > Energy saver. Source: https://source.android.com/docs/core/power/tv-standby
- Screensaver / ambient mode. Browse screens will hand over to the Google TV screensaver after idle time. Hold keep-screen-on only in the player, and make sure the player resumes correctly after the screensaver or after sleep.
- Turkey sells both the 2nd Gen (MDZ-28-AA, 2 GB/8 GB, Mali-G31, Wi-Fi 5-class) and the 3rd Gen (2 GB/32 GB, ARM G310 V2, up to 2.5 GHz, Wi-Fi 6). Vatan Bilgisayar lists both. Xiaomi's Turkish product name for the 2nd gen is 'Xiaomi TV Box S 2. Nesil 4K Ultra HD Akış Özellikli Medya Oynatıcı'. The first setup step should confirm the model at Settings > System > About (2nd gen = MiTV-AFKR0). Sources: https://www.mi.com/tr/product/xiaomi-tv-box-s-2nd-gen/specs ; https://www.mi.com/tr/product/xiaomi-tv-box-s-3rd-gen/specs ; https://www.vatanbilgisayar.com/xiaomi-mi-tv-box-s-android-2-nesil-siyah.html
- Forced OS upgrade risk mid-project. A11 units may be force-updated to A14; an Oct 2026 user report calls the update mandatory and about 1.05 GB. The QA plan needs both API levels, and storage guidance should keep at least 1.5 GB free for OTAs.
- Developer setup hardware. Add a USB-A male-to-male cable (proven to work for adb and fastboot on this box) to the dev kit. Offer it as the fallback when network ADB will not connect.
- Google TV 'Apps only mode' (official path: Settings > Accounts & Sign In > select profile > Apps only mode > Turn on). It removes recommendation clutter, which suits elderly users. The trade-off is that Search and Google Assistant stop working. Decide whether to recommend it. Source: https://support.google.com/googletv/answer/10070784
- Auto-launch / 'always open our app'. Android 10+ blocks starting activities from the background, so launching on boot or wake usually needs the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW grant; on Google TV that may only be possible via `adb shell appops`. Replacing the launcher needs disabling com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx via ADB. Android Authority reported that a Google TV update broke Projectivy's accessibility-based Home override; I could not read that article (Cloudflare) and its date and scope are unverified. The plan should not depend on intercepting Home. Pin the app first in Google TV favourites and treat auto-start as an optional experiment.
- Google TV 'basic TV' setup (no Google account) has no Play Store, so the Downloader route will not work. Check how the parents' box was set up; the fallback is USB stick + file manager, or ADB. This is generic Google TV behaviour, not verified on this unit (low confidence).
- Network path. Xiaomi confirms USB-to-Ethernet adapter support, but which adapter chipsets the firmware supports is unverified, and throughput is capped by USB 2.0 (androidpctv's 'Gigabit Ethernet' entry is misleading). List one tested adapter, or recommend 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
- Turkish-market remote. Log.com.tr's Turkish review (19 Dec 2023) confirms the Turkish unit has a Xiaomi TV key, Netflix/Prime Video/YouTube shortcuts, an apps key, an Assistant key and Home, with IR plus Bluetooth. Whether the Xiaomi TV+ service exists in Turkey is unconfirmed, so the key may do nothing. Document the remote's real behaviour on the unit in Turkish onboarding.
- On-device text-size and accessibility settings (font scale, TalkBack on Google TV). The UI should honour fontScale and provide TalkBack content descriptions for elderly users. Where Google TV 11/14 puts the text-size setting on this firmware is unverified.
- In-app voice search. The Assistant key is system-handled (KEYCODE_ASSIST and VOICE_ASSIST are 'not delivered to applications'). Whether an in-app SpeechRecognizer with RECORD_AUDIO can use the remote's mic on this firmware is unverified. Decide whether channel search is D-pad only.
- Remote pairing and battery recovery steps (2x AAA, not included). Families will need a Turkish 're-pair the remote' instruction. The exact Xiaomi re-pair key combination was not verified.
- An ExoPlayer live-HLS buffer budget sized to about 1 GB of free RAM (a few segments, not 100+ MB), with player release in onStop. This is distinct from the VOD-oriented figures in Google's memory guide.
